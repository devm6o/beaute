Hello, and welcome. This blog is a place to write down things I learn while
working with software, so that future me (and maybe you) can find them again.

## How it is organized

- **Sections** group posts by subject. Every post belongs to exactly one
  section, such as [Linux](/linux/) or [Rust](/rust/). The *Older* and *Newer*
  links at the bottom of a post stay inside its section.
- **Tags** cut across sections. A post about TLS in the Linux section and a
  post about Rust could both be tagged `security`.
- **Search** looks through titles, tags, and the full text of every post.

> NOTE: Posts are short and practical on purpose. If something is wrong or out
> of date, please let me know.
